Novella: Kotlin native android application made in Android Studio.
A simple Android app in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. This can be used to keep track of known persons by adding names to a list, tracking progress of a task, or writing ideas as themes. Difficulties come from class restrictions that while allowing for additions still limits the actual assignment indirectly as creating a vast code-base makes it hard to find or follow the notes.

Voice Mail: Rust and C++ program designed in VS Code.
An in-progress application for lazy students that uses audio transcription and local language models to summarize, in variable depths, different lectures, presentations, and documents for the person to understand the gist without excess linguistics. Difficulties come from the size of the program and the lack of knowledge of C++ and Rust. While it compiles it's heavily dependent on certain computers currently.

Ratiocentra: EPUB publication with art.
A privatized in-progress fiction that speculates future events. It attempts to do what many movies and stories lack by teaching in entertaining ways. Matching consequences of the actions provided in a utopia. Having characters do what's realistic to their personalities and changing over time. Fulfilling the stakes that are raised, staying within the rules and realities of what should be. Exploring one aspect of the world. Difficulties arise from the length of the book, as original ideas for 25 chapters are difficult to come by. Although I haven't started I believe that formatting this as an EPUB will be difficult as well as figuring what the chapter art should be.
