About

Welcome, I construct code and philosophical fiction. I enjoy making uncomfortable thought experiments to reveal subjective truths through various plots and plans. Stop-motion animation and two-dimensional art helps display the tedium of creativity contrasting the humanity lost in the brevity of reality. Audiobooks and novellas assist in imagination, originality brings controversy and education, thrill brings entertainment imminent in society. The space for boredom breeds original thought. When allowed to linger in nowhere and nothing, enough experience the mind fights battles. Comfortable entertainment breeds easy consumption, connotative afterthought warps society toward quiet perfection. Does what you observe reflect yourself, or do you reflect what you observe?

  • Figuring out ways to complicate easy things, and simplify complex things.
